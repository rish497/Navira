# NAVIRA Brand Exploration

Status: exploratory. No direction in this document is approved until the user selects it in the visual picker.

## Strategic Core

NAVIRA turns uncertain disaster information into verified, coordinated action across civilians, responders, and command teams.

Brand promise: **clarity that moves people safely**.

The identity must communicate authority under pressure without looking militaristic, alarmist, bureaucratic, or like a generic enterprise dashboard.

## Shared Mark

Concept: **the protected path**.

The mark combines two structural boundary planes with a diagonal route moving through the center. Together they form an `N` without relying on a conventional letter tile. The outer planes express protection and coordination. The route expresses movement, evacuation, dispatch, and connection between people and command.

Usage rules:

- Keep the silhouette simple enough for a map marker, reflective patch, app icon, report stamp, and equipment stencil.
- Use one flat foreground and one semantic route accent.
- Preserve clear negative space around the center route.
- Do not enclose the mark in a shield.
- Do not add cross, siren, heartbeat, radar, or lightning imagery.
- Use the full wordmark when trust and attribution matter. Use the mark alone only when NAVIRA is already established in context.

## Shared Typography

- Primary: **Urbanist**. Use for wordmark studies, display lines, section titles, map labels, and decisive actions.
- Secondary: **Google Sans**. Use for supporting interface copy, guidance, form labels, and longer reading.
- Data: a neutral tabular monospace only for coordinates, timestamps, units, incident IDs, logs, and telemetry.
- Display tracking: `-0.03em` to `-0.015em`.
- Body tracking: `0`.
- Technical labels: modest positive tracking only when the label is short and functional.

## Shared Shape and Icon Direction

- Base geometry: sharp rectangles, clipped route corners, open frames, and directional cuts.
- Radius system: 0px for structural panels, 4px for compact controls, full radius only for status indicators with a clear semantic reason.
- Icon family: squared terminals, 1.75px optical stroke, open negative space, no filled cartoon symbols.
- Every icon must clarify action, state, severity, resource type, or route meaning.
- Critical state always combines color with text, icon shape, or pattern.

## Direction 1: Signal Foundry

Thesis: NAVIRA behaves like a modern civic signal system built into the infrastructure of a city.

### Palette

| Token | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graphite | `#0F1113` | Primary substrate |
| Signal Vermilion | `#FF3B1F` | Action, route, attention |
| Bone White | `#F1EEE9` | Primary text and light surface |
| Steel | `#68737A` | Infrastructure and secondary data |
| Safety Amber | `#F4B800` | Caution only |

### Surfaces and Borders

- Matte graphite and powder-coated metal.
- Flat fills with visible compartments and 1px steel rules.
- Vermilion appears as a route, action edge, or verified transition.
- No glow. No translucent floating layers.

### Motion

- Route segments draw from source to destination.
- Verification states lock into alignment with a short mechanical settle.
- Scroll motion feels like switching between control-room channels.

### Risk

The direction can become overly severe if every surface stays dark or every action becomes red.

## Direction 2: Terrain Ledger

Thesis: NAVIRA is a shared field atlas where evidence, land, and action stay visibly connected.

### Palette

| Token | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Survey White | `#F0EEE8` | Primary paper surface |
| Carbon | `#202624` | Text and structural ink |
| Rescue Orange | `#FF5A16` | Active route and action |
| Moss Grey | `#68716C` | Terrain and secondary data |
| Contour Tan | `#C7B89E` | Topographic context |

### Surfaces and Borders

- Uncoated field paper, weatherproof map stock, graphite rub, reflective route vinyl.
- Dividers follow the logic of folds, map legends, and report blocks.
- Texture is subtle and tied to terrain, print, or field equipment.

### Motion

- Map folds open to reveal the next level of evidence.
- Contour lines remain still while a verified route resolves across them.
- Content transitions feel like turning a report sheet, not sliding a card carousel.

### Risk

The editorial warmth can feel less immediate in a night command center unless the dark operational extension is carefully defined.

## Direction 3: Night Beacon

Thesis: NAVIRA is the single readable signal that remains when conditions become dark, noisy, and unstable.

### Palette

| Token | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beacon Black | `#0B0D0D` | Primary low-light substrate |
| Signal Chartreuse | `#C7FF18` | Active route, focus, confirmation |
| Warm White | `#F2F0E9` | Primary text |
| Graphite | `#333738` | Secondary surface |
| Alert Red | `#FF493D` | Danger only |

### Surfaces and Borders

- Matte black aluminum, reflective chartreuse vinyl, dark concrete, restrained broadcast raster.
- A rigid modular grid carries alignment. The interface does not imitate a retro terminal.
- Luminance is created through contrast and reflective color, not outer glow.

### Motion

- Information resolves in block groups, then holds still for reading.
- Role changes act like switching a public information channel.
- Blink is reserved for unresolved or actively changing state and must be stoppable.

### Risk

Chartreuse and broadcast structure can drift into cyberpunk or teletext nostalgia if texture becomes decorative.

## Decision Criteria

Choose the direction that best balances:

1. Trust for civilians.
2. Precision for responders and command teams.
3. Distinctive recognition at a glance.
4. Range across light, dark, field, print, map, and dense operational surfaces.
5. Clarity under stress and imperfect conditions.

The selected direction will be refined into the durable `DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/design.json`. The other directions remain exploration only.
