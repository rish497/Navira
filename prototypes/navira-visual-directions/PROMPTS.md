# Generated Brand Board Prompts

Built-in image generation was used for all three project-bound brand boards.

## Signal Foundry

Create a premium 3x3 NAVIRA identity board for a disaster-response technology platform. Build a civic-industrial visual world from modern civil-defense broadcast rooms, transit wayfinding, and equipment labels. Use a protected-path `N` mark formed by two boundary planes and a clear diagonal route. Palette: graphite, signal vermilion, bone white, steel, and semantic safety amber. Show the logo, construction, palette, type, route application, responder equipment, verification strip, icons, and the tagline `CLARITY MOVES FIRST`. Keep the board sparse, high-contrast, sharp, and free of generic SaaS UI, glow, glass, shield imagery, or emergency clipart.

## Terrain Ledger

Create a premium 3x3 NAVIRA identity board for a disaster-response technology platform. Build an editorial-industrial world from emergency field atlases, hydrology survey folios, annotated topographic rescue plans, and civic identity manuals. Use the same protected-path `N` mark. Palette: survey white, carbon, rescue orange, moss grey, and contour tan. Show the logo, construction, palette and materials, type, folded evacuation map, verified report stamp, field patch, contour-based icon direction, and the tagline `EVIDENCE INTO ACTION`. Keep it tactile, calm, high-contrast, and free of generic dashboards or decorative emergency symbols.

## Night Beacon

Create a premium 3x3 NAVIRA identity board for a disaster-response technology platform. Build a low-light operational identity from public warning broadcast systems, airport emergency wayfinding, reflective field markings, and disciplined block-mosaic graphics. Use the same protected-path `N` mark. Palette: beacon black, signal chartreuse, warm white, graphite, and tiny semantic alert red. Show the logo, construction, material chips, type, night evacuation broadcast, reflective patch, alert strip, icon and block direction, and the tagline `ONE SIGNAL. CLEAR ACTION.` Keep luminance controlled through contrast, not glow. Avoid cyberpunk, hacker, or retro-terminal cosplay.
