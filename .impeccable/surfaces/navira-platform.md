# NAVIRA platform surface contract

## Selection record

The user requested implementation using the established design system after the visual direction round. Signal Foundry was the lead direction in that round and is promoted here as the implementation source of truth.

## THESIS

NAVIRA is civic signal infrastructure for a disaster in motion. It replaces the visual language of a generic SaaS dashboard with a hard-edged operating picture built from routes, evidence, resources, infrastructure, and human consequence.

## OWN-WORLD

- Graphite command infrastructure, bone field surfaces, signal vermilion, safety amber, and steel gray.
- Urbanist provides the wide, direct typographic voice.
- The protected-path N mark and route geometry identify the product across public and operational views.
- Rules, ledgers, rails, bands, coordinates, and map boundaries provide the material language.
- Original disaster photography supplies environmental reality on the public landing surface.

## STORY

The public landing page moves from urgency to a shared operating picture, then explains verification, decision, and coordination. The product follows the same sequence through command, mapping, evidence, evacuation, dispatch, resources, infrastructure, simulation, timeline, analytics, and civilian guidance.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

The landing viewport is split into a tightly cropped proposition and a full-height command frame. The headline reads in three broad lines. A real disaster image carries the environmental weight, while an incident plate and route overlay make the software purpose immediately legible.

The command viewport opens with a concise situation statement and an asymmetric 8/4 operating grid. The live regional map is dominant; the priority queue, population at risk, teams deployed, infrastructure strip, and resource ticker form one connected instrument.

## FORM

- GPT Taste concept-roll seed: `786` (`Cropped command frame`, `Urbanist`, `Resource ticker`, `Infrastructure strip`, `Layer rail`, `signal pulse`, `panel handoff`).
- Square corners and hard grid alignment.
- High contrast and visibly authored hierarchy.
- Dense operational surfaces with familiar controls; spacious public storytelling.
- Vermilion is reserved for active risk and direct action.
- Motion uses route tracing, signal pulse, and panel handoff only where state or sequence benefits.
- All operational values are labeled as an illustrative scenario.

## Quality reference

The Signal Foundry board at `prototypes/navira-visual-directions/assets/signal-foundry-brand-board.png` is a critique reference for palette, lettering, route geometry, and industrial tone. The product was built code-led from this contract rather than as a pixel reproduction of that board.
